# Secret Santa

A private gift exchange for friends and family, organised in rooms.

## Language

**Room**:
A group gathering for one Secret Santa draw.

**Participant**:
A person taking part in a room's gift exchange, identified to the group by a name.
_Avoid_: User, account

**Room owner**:
The participant who creates a room and has authority to start its draw.
_Avoid_: Admin, host

**Room code**:
The code shared with people so they can find and join a room.

**Draw**:
The one-time event that assigns gift recipients to participants in a room.
_Avoid_: Matching round

**Gift recipient**:
The person a participant is assigned to buy a gift for. Each participant's assignment is private to that participant.
_Avoid_: Match, partner
